Your shortlist. Your questions. Your decision.

The planned assistant will collect shop answers. You’ll choose what to buy; negotiation and purchasing are outside the first version.

You’ll approve shops before calls. The app will show names and phone numbers so you can choose up to five shops.

Shops will be asked for catalogs. You’ll provide your WhatsApp number so shops can send catalogs directly. Delivery depends on the shop; an agreement is not confirmation of receipt.

Gaps stay visible. The app is planned to flag unclear and missing answers, keep partial results, and let you retry failed calls.
