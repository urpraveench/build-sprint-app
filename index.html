Keep the details. Make a clearer choice.

The planned companion follows your purchase from the first questions to the final price you record.

  1. 1

    Bring your needs into focus

    Set your budget and priorities. Prepare questions for shop visits, or go straight to offers you have already collected.

  2. 2

    Collect, then check

    Keep each shop’s notes and photos together. Review the details the companion captured, with the original source beside them. Unclear answers stay marked.

  3. 3

    Compare against what matters

    See differences in models, costs, warranty and fit. Check important missing details before choosing. Add another shop if no offer fits.

  4. 4

    Prepare to bargain, then record your purchase

    Use bargaining guidance when current, comparable quotes support it. You bargain and buy at the shop, then save what you bought and the final price paid.
