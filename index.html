From shop visits to offers you can compare.

Prepare before visiting shops, or start with notes and photos you already have. Either way, compare using your needs and budget.

  1. 1

    Prepare for your purchase

    Enter the product, its purpose, and your budget. Get questions to ask every shop, add your own, and select nearby shops with a suggested visit order.

  2. 2

    Keep each shop’s details together

    Visit the shops and save notes, product photos, or screenshots under each one. Confirm the model, price, and details the app understood.

  3. 3

    Compare, check, and choose

    Compare different models against your needs. Check missing costs and specifications, use bargaining guidance when quotes support it, and record what you bought and paid.

Your needs. Your evidence. Your decision.

You collect the shop information and check the product in person. The companion will help you compare and prepare to bargain; you decide what to buy and purchase it at the shop.

Compare what matters to you. Compare different brands and models by the specifications that matter for your needs. If none fits, keep looking.

Keep the source in view. Keep notes and photos attached to each offer. Shopkeeper claims stay labelled, and unclear details need your confirmation.

Check before you choose. Missing costs, fit, warranty, and service details stay visible. Bargaining guidance needs current, comparable quotes; without enough evidence, no price is invented.
