# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Mobile app, supported by a web landing page and a planned comparison-history dashboard.

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, published with Convex static hosting using npm run deploy.

## Users

People in India comparing household appliances sold by nearby shops. The first user is Praveen, beginning with kitchen chimneys; refrigerators and washing machines also fit the first version.

## Product Purpose

Save the time spent visiting or calling several shops by collecting comparable answers in one place. Success means a buyer can make a faster, better-informed purchase decision.

## Positioning

The assistant asks the same useful questions of up to five user-approved shops, requests catalogs through the user's WhatsApp number, and turns the answers into a comparison without inventing missing details.

## Operating Context

In the mobile app, the user starts with one appliance type, their area, requirements, and a WhatsApp number. They approve shops before calls begin, then review each call outcome and a model-by-model comparison.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- V1 covers requirements, shop discovery and approval, calls, catalog requests, and comparison.
- Negotiation and purchasing are outside V1.
- Missing answers must appear as “Not provided,” and unclear answers must be flagged.
- Calling, shop discovery, languages, and automatic WhatsApp catalog handling remain undecided.
- No pricing has been confirmed.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product scope: `IDEA_SCOPE.md`
- V1 behavior and boundaries: `PLAN.md`
- No customer testimonials, verified savings claims, final product name, or brand assets are available.

## Product Principles

- Keep the buyer in control of which shops are contacted.
- Make offers easy to compare without hiding units or price inclusions.
- Preserve incomplete results and say plainly when information is missing.
- Use AI to reduce repetitive calls, while leaving the buying decision to the user.

## Landing-page direction

The main action directs visitors to the mobile app. Calls are started and managed from the mobile app. The web dashboard is planned for saved comparisons and history. No app-store links or dashboard URL have been supplied; show coming-soon availability until real destinations exist.

## Availability and claims

The app and dashboard are planned, not usable yet. The current primary action opens a sample comparison. Do not offer downloads until a real app link exists. Catalog requests do not guarantee delivery; missing answers and failed calls must stay visible. Launch date, supported phones, service areas, call languages, and pricing are unannounced.
